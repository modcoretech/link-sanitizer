Link Sanitizer Settings

Customize how the extension cleans your URLs

Automation

Automate common tasks to speed up your workflow and save time.

Auto-Sanitize Links New

Automatically clean all links before opening them. When enabled, every link you click will be sanitized to remove tracking parameters before the page loads.

Note: This intercepts all navigation. If you experience issues with certain websites, you can disable this feature.
Auto-Paste from Clipboard

Automatically paste content from your clipboard when you open the extension popup. Perfect for quickly cleaning URLs you've just copied.

Privacy: Only URLs are processed. The extension reads your clipboard only when you open it.
Auto-Copy Results

Automatically copy the cleaned URL to your clipboard after sanitizing. No need to click the copy button.

History & Privacy

Control what data is stored locally on your device.

Keep Cleaning History

Store a local history of cleaned URLs. This helps you track what you've cleaned and quickly re-access previous URLs.

Privacy: History is stored only on your device and never sent to any server.
History Limit

Maximum number of URLs to keep in history.

Show Notifications

Display toast notifications when URLs are cleaned, copied, or when errors occur.

Advanced Options

Fine-tune how URLs are processed and cleaned.

Preserve Hash Parameters

Keep tracking parameters that appear after the # (hash) symbol in URLs. Some single-page applications use hash parameters for navigation.

Example: example.com#section?utm_source=ad → hash params will be preserved
Case-Sensitive Matching

Match parameter names exactly as typed. When disabled, "UTM_SOURCE" and "utm_source" are treated the same.

Block Redirect Trackers

Remove common redirect parameters used by URL shorteners and social media platforms to track clicks.

Custom Tracking Parameters

Add your own tracking parameters to block. The extension already blocks 50+ common trackers by default.

Examples: my_tracker, internal_id, ref_code
View Default Blocked Parameters (50+)

Data Management

Manage your local data and reset the extension to defaults.

Privacy Notice: All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's sync storage. Nothing is sent to external servers except when you use the "Shorten URL" feature (which connects to TinyURL).
